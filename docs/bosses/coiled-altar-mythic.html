The Coiled Altar

MythicHeroic

Zul'jan alone, then Hex Lord Malacrass alone, then both together. In the intermission Zul'jan takes double damage (Ghastly Regeneration) and Malacrass cannot be hurt. Spiteful Soulcoilers spawn across the room, and SimulationCraft has no positions, so cleave on them is optimistic.

  • 2 targets
  • 7 add waves
  • Melee move 70.7 s
  • Ranged move 68.3 s
  • From 30 kills
