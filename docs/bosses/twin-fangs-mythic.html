The Venomous Abyss / boss 6 of 8

The Twin Fangs

MythicHeroic

Two bosses, cleaved the whole fight, on a 155-second cycle. Each cycle brings three Spawn of Vexhul and ten Broodlings of Ithraz. In each intermission Ithraz is out of reach for about 15 seconds while the raid crosses the room.

  • Kill length 6:34
  • 2 targets
  • 10 add waves
  • Melee move 20.3 s
  • Ranged move 57.8 s
  • From 30 kills
