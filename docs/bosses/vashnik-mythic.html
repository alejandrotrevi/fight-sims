The Venomous Abyss

Vashnik the Malignant

MythicHeroic
30 kills measuredkill 5:43 (median of 1000)1 target9 add wavesmelee move 8.1 sranged move 27.3 s

One boss with a lot of add damage. Each Imbibe (every 84 seconds) releases eight Shrouded Venoms and three Burning Venoms on mythic, and all of them have to die before they reach the center. About a third of the raid's damage goes into them.

The profile

Paste this into Raidbots' Script Footer (SimC Expert Mode), with Fight Style Casting Patchwerk and 1 boss. Step by step.

"Into adds" is the share of damage that went into adds rather than the bosses: in the sim, and for real players of the same spec in the 30 kills (average per player: raids put some players on adds and leave others on the boss, so many real players put 0% into adds). The adds' exposure (count × alive time) is calibrated ×0.905 so that the sim matches the real players; the measured counts and alive times are listed with each wave above.
