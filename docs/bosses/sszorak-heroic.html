MythicHeroic

One boss on a fixed 138-second cycle (mythic: 127). Every Howling Maelstrom he digs in and takes 30% more damage for 25 seconds. Most movement comes from targeted debuffs (Venomous Surge, Raging Crosswinds) that send a few players out at a time.
