The Venomous Abyss / boss 8 of 8

Ula'tek

MythicHeroic

Ula'tek and her tail (Gore Rattle) are cleaved together in phase 1. After each Rage her Venomous Heart is exposed for 20 seconds and takes double damage: measured from the logs as exactly 2.00 times what Ula'tek takes from the same player and ability. In phases 2 and 3 she is gone and each half of the raid kills its own adds, so those adds count half. The Weakened Doomscales take +100% damage, which simc can only apply to a target, so one per half is simulated as a target.

  • Kill length 9:25
  • 4 targets
  • 17 add waves
  • Melee move 98.9 s
  • Ranged move 117.5 s
  • From 30 kills
