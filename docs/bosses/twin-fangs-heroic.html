The Venomous Abyss / boss 6 of 8

The Twin Fangs

MythicHeroic

Two bosses, cleaved the whole fight. Each cycle brings three Spawn of Vexhul; heroic has no Broodlings. In each intermission Ithraz is out of reach for 10-20 seconds while the raid crosses the room.

  • Kill length 6:00
  • 2 targets
  • 4 add waves
  • Melee move 24.6 s
  • Ranged move 58.6 s
  • From 30 kills

Profile

# The Twin Fangs (heroic) - measured fight profile for SimulationCraft / Raidbots
# Raidbots: Fight Style = Casting Patchwerk (NOT Patchwerk: it deletes raid events), Number of Bosses = 1,
