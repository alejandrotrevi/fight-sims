The Venomous Abyss / boss 3 of 8

Vashnik the Malignant

MythicHeroic

One boss with a lot of add damage. Each Imbibe (every 84 seconds) releases five Shrouded Venoms and two Burning Venoms on heroic (mythic: eight and three), and all of them have to die before they reach the center.

  • Kill length 4:26
  • 1 target
  • 6 add waves
  • Melee move 6.4 s
  • Ranged move 17.7 s
  • From 30 kills
