Ula'tek

Ula'tek and her tail (Gore Rattle) are cleaved together in phase 1. After each Rage her Venomous Heart is exposed for 20 seconds and takes double damage: measured from the logs as exactly 2.00 times what Ula'tek takes from the same player and ability. In phases 2 and 3 she is gone and each half of the raid kills its own adds, so those adds count half. The Weakened Doomscales take +100% damage, which simc can only apply to a target, so one per half is simulated as a target.

"Into adds" is the share of damage that went into adds rather than the bosses: in the sim, and for real players of the same spec in the 30 kills (average per player: raids put some players on adds and leave others on the boss, so many real players put 0% into adds). The adds' exposure (count × alive time) is calibrated ×0.2 so that the sim matches the real players; the measured counts and alive times are listed with each wave above.

Known gap. Even after calibration, the sample characters put on average 11 percentage points more of their damage into adds than real players of the same specs (the calibration is at its lowest allowed factor). Something in this fight that the profile cannot express, such as downtime the movement measurement misses, still differs. Treat results that depend on AoE with some caution: they will look a little better than they are.
