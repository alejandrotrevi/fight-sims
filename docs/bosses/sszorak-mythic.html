The Venomous Abyss

Sszorak

MythicHeroic
30 kills measuredkill 5:54 (median of 1000)1 target0 add wavesmelee move 11.1 sranged move 43.1 s

One boss on a fixed 127-second cycle. Every Howling Maelstrom he digs in and takes 30% more damage for 25 seconds. Most movement comes from targeted debuffs (Venomous Surge, Raging Crosswinds) that send a few players out at a time.

The profile

Paste this into Raidbots' Script Footer (SimC Expert Mode), with Fight Style Casting Patchwerk and 1 boss. Step by step.

# Sszorak (mythic) - measured fight profile for SimulationCraft / Raidbots
# Raidbots: Fight Style = Casting Patchwerk (NOT Patchwerk: it deletes raid events), Number of Bosses = 1,
