Three explorers with separate health. Two are kept in cleave and the third is pulled more than 30 yards away (all three together take 99% less damage). Which one stands apart changes from kill to kill. In the 30 heroic kills two explorers were taking damage 51% of the time and all three 35%, so the sim has two targets. The empowered-explorer phases come in random order and count as one kind of phase here.
